Recycle XP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Recycle XP is a free app made by George Klonos. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled. The short version: everything stays on your phone.

What the app uses

  • Camera. The app uses your camera only when you tap "Log a recycle", to take a photo of the item you are recycling.
  • Photos you take in the app. These are saved in the app's own private storage on your device, so you can see them in your history.
  • Your progress. Your XP, level, streaks and daily log count are saved on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or ask for your name, email or any personal details.
  • It does not upload your photos or any other data to a server.
  • It does not share or sell any data to third parties.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not access your location, contacts or the photos in your gallery.

Deleting your data

All data is stored on your device only. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Recycle XP is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. Since the app collects no personal data at all, no information about anyone is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If future versions of the app add features that handle data differently, such as accounts, leaderboards or cloud features, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the date above will change.
